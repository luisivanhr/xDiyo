# V47: selected-value lineage and original-round follow-up

V47 is a **review-only package**, not an integration approval. It retains all 61,488 fixtures, 794 historical holds and 368,928 field records; all field integration and model-input approval flags remain false. It supersedes the V44 and V45 compact mappings.

## What changed

* **Nine Bulgarian lineage mismatches:** for fixtures 130879, 130885 and 130890, `candidate_value` now matches the selected phase-round clear, phase key and stage label. `v45_v44_candidate_value_history` and associated history columns retain the former 1/2/3 and prior labels. `v47_selected_explicit_clear` marks the three numeric clears. The full-population selected mapping and lineage have zero selected-value mismatches.
* **MLS series ordinals:** the compact mapping now retains `v45_series_game_number` for all 60 MLS best-of-three matches. It remains separate from `v44_selected_leg_number`; the source lineage is also in `v45_mls_series_game_lineage_REVIEW.csv`.
* **Greek playoff draw:** 12 provider-selected values are explicitly labelled as a SofaScore-convention choice with competing reports. [PAOK-focused reporting](https://www.paokmania.gr/podosfairo/protathlima/188713-programma-ton-play-offs) and [an APE-MPE-attributed March 15 report](https://www.drt915.gr/me-panathinaiko-stin-premiera-ton-plei-of-o-asteras-tripolis/) currently agree with the provider rounds; [another March 15 report](https://www.iefimerida.gr/spor/super-league-paok-aek-plei-olo-programma) gives different rounds 6/7 and 8/10. The accessible current texts can share draw lineage, and historical page versions are not frozen. This strengthens the provider selection but does not prove which report was erroneous or revised. All three links and differing numbers remain in `v45_greek_12_original_draw_conflict_REVIEW.csv` and the field lineage.
* **14 Australian 2020/21 conflicts:** each now has a specific season-round conflict gate, the prior gate, original club claim, existing candidate, source, identity basis and repeated-pair count. The four inferred prose claims are flagged as inferred; four cases have repeated ordered pairs in the target inventory. See `v46_australia_14_original_draw_conflicts_REVIEW.csv`. Thirteen cases other than 73239 remain competing claims, not proven replacements. No provider event was observed in this pass.
* **Australian 73239:** the [November original release](https://wswanderersfc.com.au/news/wanderers-open-a-league-202021-season/) explicitly assigns the unique ordered pair on January 19 to Round 6, while the [January 18 league preview](https://aleagues.com.au/news/matchweek-4-preview-central-coast-mariners-v-western-sydney-wanderers/) labels the same dated event Matchweek 4. V47 selects **original Round 6** in `v46_selected_season_round`; the previous 4 and later playing-week evidence remain in the field lineage. Both sources are current versions of historical pages; the source conflict gate remains held pending independent audit.
* **Melbourne City V13:** all 26 records with source numbers carry a warning that the 1–40 namespace is unverified and must not be compared to or substituted for league rounds. Twenty-five happen to differ numerically; this is not counted as 25 proven conflicts.
* **Phase-key scope:** the native consumer has no hard-coded dependency on a field literally named `phase_key`, but [history.py](C:/Users/luisi/Documents/Programming/Python/xDiyo/src/xdiyo_analytics/features/history.py:4) says stage belongs in round-group keys when needed, and default feature/config round keys group by competition, season and round. Thus repeated **phase-local** numbers need a scoped stage key for any grouping that uses them; unique cumulative round values may not. This does not establish the final adapter configuration. V47 carries forward the existing 28 Greek 2021/22, 54 Greek 2023/24–2025/26 and 114 Danish 2019/20–2020/21 source proposals into `v47_selected_phase_key`; 165 selected keys change, the rest already matched. Their country, season, group, counts and one-row-per-fixture scope were checked, previous keys and lineage preserved, and none approved. The remaining 83 literal unresolved keys are separate auxiliary namespace issues; their necessity depends on the eventual consumer and cannot establish a wrong numeric round by themselves.
