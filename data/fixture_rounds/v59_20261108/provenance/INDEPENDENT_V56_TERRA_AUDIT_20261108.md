## Published round namespace and anomaly

The 156 source-table claims cover labels 1–40 and form coherent groups in the limited sense that no team appears twice under the same label. For much of the early schedule, even-numbered groups span weekend fixtures while intervening groups carry one or two midweek fixtures. This is a source-published scheduling convention, not 156 match IDs, and two different source dates can refer to the same event over revisions. It is not shown to be SofaScore's convention, nor a demonstrably equivalent remapping of the club/weekly 1–26 convention.

Concrete cases with exact target pair/date illustrate why the conventions must remain distinct: Adelaide–Sydney 73175 is Round 16 in the November club narrative but 26 in the contemporaneously dated league-table page; Victory–City 73197 is 12 in the original club narrative but 20 in the league-table page. Exact source wording and vintage should accompany each, rather than asserting one is automatically erroneous. League page publication date alone does not prove every table value's historic vintage.

There is also an internal anomaly: Perth–Western United appears as league table **Round 15 on 5 May**, on both clubs' current pages, while other contemporaneous May 5 table matches are labelled 36. The original FFA PDF also schedules this ordered pair on May 5, so that comparison does **not** show its date was later revised. It does show the table label is out of its usual chronological pattern, making an automatic conversion especially unsafe. Preserve the claim and anomaly rather than silently discarding it or assuming its cause.
